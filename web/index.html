Every finding is reported with the evidence behind it and the action it implies. The score is a triage aid — the reason codes are the output that matters, because a held drawdown has to be explainable to the customer, the broker and the ombudsman.

  • Payment instrument — where the money is actually going, measured against every account this supplier has been paid at before.
  • Document forensics — patches over the payment block, recoverable original text, font drift, incremental saves.
  • File metadata — the toolchain that produced the PDF, and what it stopped carrying.
  • Content & business logic — arithmetic, ABN, contacts, numbering, stage schedule.
  • Differential — this document against a known-good one from the same supplier.
